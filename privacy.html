Privacy policy

BigCatWise is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. We collect as little information as possible, and this page explains what we collect and why, including how advertising works on the site.

Who we are

This website, bigcatwise.com, is owned and operated by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC ("we", "us"). You can contact us about privacy at joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form messages. If you use our contact form, the name, email address and message you type are sent to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third party form service that passes them to our inbox and may process technical data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. Do not include sensitive personal information in your message.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.
  • Games. Our games save your best score only in your own browser (local storage) so you can try to beat it. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and you can clear it by clearing your browser's site data. The games need no sign up and collect no personal data.
  • Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It is cookieless and does not collect personal data or track you across sites.
  • Advertising. Google AdSense may collect and use cookie and device data to show and measure ads; see Advertising below.

We use contact details only to reply to you and to handle your request. We do not sell, rent or share them for marketing.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations; see GitHub's privacy statement. The Fredoka and Nunito fonts are served from this site itself, not from Google Fonts or any other third party, so loading a page sends no font requests elsewhere.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first, cookieless service, to count page visits. It does not set cookies, use local storage or fingerprint visitors, and it does not track you across other sites. See Cloudflare Web Analytics for details.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads, and the AdSense code loads on the pages of this site. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, which can include personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and manage ad settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message provided through Google's AdSense and consent tools, so you can accept or decline personalised ads, and you can change that choice at any time using the control it provides. You can also opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com or aboutads.info, or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how ads display. See also our advertising and affiliate disclosure. Our own analytics stay cookieless, as described above.

Cookies and affiliates

Apart from the cookies Google and its partners may set for advertising, this site does not set cookies of its own. There are no affiliate links or affiliate cookies on this site. If that changes, we will update this policy and our disclaimer before it goes live.

Retention and your choices

We keep contact messages only as long as needed to respond and keep a reasonable record of the conversation. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you sent us by emailing joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

Children

BigCatWise is a general educational resource. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not target ads to children, and Google ads on the site are not intended to be directed to children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss law and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information. Contact us through the contact form or by email and we will respond within a reasonable time. We do not sell personal information.

Governing law
