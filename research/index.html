For students and researchers: the papers behind BigCatWise

Last reviewed 8 October 2026

Every BigCatWise fact article cites its sources. This page gathers the peer reviewed papers we rely on in one place, grouped by topic, with full references and DOIs that we have checked against Crossref. It is a good starting point for a school report or a literature search.

How to use this list

  • Click a DOI to open the paper's official page. Many abstracts are free to read; full text may need a library login.
  • Each entry links to the BigCatWise article that explains the findings in plain English.
  • For species facts such as weights and population estimates, our main source is the IUCN SSC Cat Specialist Group species pages, linked from each species page.
