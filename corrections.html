Corrections policy

We aim to be accurate and to fix mistakes openly. This page explains how to report an error and what we do about it.

How to report an error

Use the contact form. Please give the page address, what you think is wrong, and a source if you have one.

What we do

  1. We check the report against the primary sources listed on the page, such as the IUCN SSC Cat Specialist Group, the IUCN Red List, zoos, conservation organisations and peer reviewed research.
  2. If we find an error we correct the page, and the page's "last updated" date changes to the date of the correction.
  3. For a significant factual correction we add a short note on the page saying what changed and when.
  4. If the report is mistaken, we leave the page as it is and may reply with the source.

Our standards

Every factual claim is supported by the sources listed on its page, and we do not invent statistics or quotes. BigCatWise does not accuse or name private individuals; if news events are discussed, they are described in general terms from cited sources. If you are mentioned or pictured and want something changed, tell us through the contact form and we will review it promptly. Content is educational only and is not professional advice; see our disclaimer.
